The Gilded Standard
Wealth you can feel in the hand. Gold is the currency of attention, iron is the structure that holds it, and every certificate is worth framing.

I
Gold is earned, not sprinkled
Gold leaf marks money, the one primary action on screen, and moments of wealth: charters, buyouts, the closing bell. If everything is gold, nothing is.
II
Iron frames, lacquer holds
The ground is black lacquer. Iron is structure: frames, rivets, secondary controls. No more cream page by default — paper is reserved for documents.
III
Paper is a document
Bond paper appears only as things you own: share certificates, deeds, the final ledger. It always carries guilloché, a seal, and a signature.
